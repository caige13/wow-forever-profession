CURRENT READ

Engineering is a utility contender. “Everyone needs it” is not proven. Shared campsite buffs, tradeable gear and alternate device versions mean the profession that makes an item may not be the profession your character needs. See the reasoning.

Reading the timeline: skill = profession skill; character = required player level. Unverified gates stay unnumbered. Beta item values may change or scale.

EN

Primary · crafting

Engineering

Beta evidence

Bombs, movement devices, unusual combat tools, ranged equipment, and field services. The leading utility candidate; an automatic best-in-slot profession is not established.

BONUS & EXCLUSIVITY

The confirmed camp contribution is a service, not a personal stat increase. No verified flat personal bonus is assigned here.

    Anarchist’s Workbench

    A workspace for recipes that require it.

Selected useful examples
Character 13 → 28 → 41 → 52

Bomb satchels

Copper, Bronze, Iron and Dark Iron thrown weapons add splash damage. These are equipment-level checkpoints, not profession skill requirements.

Character 40

Compact Critter Carrier

Lets group Hunters access stabled pets for three minutes.

Character 45

Tinkerbox devices

Nitro Boosts lists up to +140% movement for up to 8 seconds; Teleport lists a 20-yard jump. Magnetic Displacement pushes both parties apart and has target restrictions.

Character 55

SAF-T / EZ-Thro versions

Alternate Nitro, Teleport and displacement devices appear in the data. SAF-T Nitro Boosts is shown as bind-on-equip; complete use restrictions still need live verification.

Character 60

Gnomish Poultryizer

A chicken transformation with a possible reversal; PvP limits and reliability need testing.

WHO SHOULD LOOK CLOSELY · ANALYSIS

Most interesting for PvP players and builds that lose damage or survival to movement. Paladins and other melee builds may value gap-closing; healers may value an escape. That is an inference, not a measured ranking.

DO YOU NEED THE PROFESSION?

Check the exact device. A crafted, tradeable version can reduce the need to be an Engineer yourself. Compare charges, cooldowns, failures, equipment slots and profession requirements before committing.

What is still unverified?

No verified raid-wide Engineering requirement. Do not import Wrath glove tinkers, Classic bomb assumptions, or unrestricted battleground use.

THE SHARED PROGRESSION TRACK

Build a camp that fills your group’s gaps.

Blizzard confirms one object per player, a shared one-hour object cooldown, three slots at a Basic Campfire and upgrades to five or ten. Camp buffs last an hour and do not stack with the corresponding class buffs. Official rules ↗

300

The final listed camp tier supports advanced crafting or gathering. It often retains the first object’s buff.

For a small leveling party

Pick the buffs your classes cannot already supply, then add repairs or crafting access. If nobody brings Kings, the Fishing bowl is worth a look; if nobody brings Fortitude, check First Aid.

For an organized raid

A matching class buff can remove the camp buff’s marginal value. Shared repairs and crafting access may need only a few providers. This is group planning, not a reason for everyone to choose the same two professions.

WHAT A PROFESSION KEEPS FOR ITSELF

Almost nothing is locked to the crafter.

Classic put the gate on the item: Requires Engineering (300) to equip. Forever’s datamined beta moves away from that. Crafted output is overwhelmingly bind-on-equip and usable by anyone. One reward category is a genuine exception, and a long list of widely repeated claims could not be corroborated at all.

THE FINDING

If you are choosing a profession to unlock gear only you can wear, the current beta does not support that plan. Engineering — the profession most defined by self-only gadgets in Classic — is explicitly included in the bind-on-equip finding. The one confirmed personal reward is the Certification title at skill 300.

Beta / datamined

Enchanting relics are gated by class, not by profession. Idols (Druid), Librams (Paladin) and Totems (Shaman) restrict who may equip them by class. Any enchanter can craft them, and holding Enchanting is not required to use one.

Profession-exclusive, but not written on the item

Official announcement
  • Camp objects. Three per profession, the first at skill 20. Holding the profession is required to craft the object; the resulting buff is then shared with everyone who sits at the fire. Whether a non-practitioner can place a traded object is not stated in the sources checked. See camps & buffs →
  • Legacy tree perks. Working Overtime, Bountiful Harvest, Luremaster and Master Chef sit in the Professions tab and modify your own crafting and gathering rather than producing a tradeable item.

RECOMMENDATIONS · INFERENCE, NOT SIMULATION

There will likely be a meta. It is not solved yet.

Optimize around what you can actually use, what only you can obtain, and the fights you intend to play. The class shortlist below identifies candidates to test, not a definitive best pair.

PvP hypothesis

Engineering has the clearest utility case.

Movement, displacement and control can decide a fight. Its lead shrinks if equivalent devices are available without the profession. Failures and mode restrictions could also change the result.

Device evidence ↗

Raid hypothesis

Exclusive throughput must be measured.

Compare real personal stat bonuses, usable gear and damage gained from movement. Alchemy’s strong consumables do not prove Alchemy is best on the player drinking them.

Consumable evidence ↗

Leveling evidence

Some personal crafts already matter.

Leatherworking has checked bind-on-pickup leveling chests. Tailoring’s checked Earthenweave Vest is bind-on-equip. Those access rules can change the profession decision.

Item binding evidence ↗

The question that decides the Engineering meta

Can a non-Engineer obtain an equivalent movement/control tool with the same cooldown, reliability and equipment cost? SAF-T and EZ-Thro variants are present, but their existence alone does not settle that question. Do not pay to level a profession based only on a tooltip name.

Practical choices you can make now

  1. Progress the secondary skills. Cooking, First Aid and Fishing offer support without using a primary profession slot.
  2. Choose your supply model. Engineering + Mining, Alchemy + Herbalism, and Leatherworking + Skinning are material-oriented pairings. Two crafts can make sense if you buy or otherwise source materials; these are economic choices, not proven combat rankings.
  3. Coordinate camp coverage. Prioritize missing buffs and services, not duplicates of effects your group already has.
  4. Keep the endgame choice open. Confirm finished-item binding, skill-to-use requirements, shared cooldowns, and what happens after unlearning a profession.

Bonuses outside the profession itself

Legacy beta data

Legacy perks can change the convenience of your setup. These are separate point choices, not automatic bonuses for choosing a profession. Blizzard announces a 16-point spend cap at launch. Official Legacy overview ↗

Camp upkeep

The beta lists Field Guide at up to 25% less camp-object cooldown, Permanence at up to 50% longer camp buffs, and Reagent Economy removing tier-one camp reagent costs.

Crafting & gathering

Working Overtime improves skill-up chances; Bountiful Harvest increases scarce gathered materials. Master Chef, Luremaster and Gourmand support food production, fishing and food-buff duration.

Highest listed values may require multiple ranks. Legacy data & ranks ↗

Avoid borrowed metas: this guide does not assume Classic Engineering supremacy, TBC drums, Wrath gathering passives or later-expansion enchants apply to Forever. No DPS percentages or BiS labels are invented.

Personal profession bonuses: still an evidence gap

A Forever overview ↗ reports stat bonuses for professions with Engineering as an exception. Its full details could not be inspected here, and a complete matching set of personal-bonus tooltips was not verified. That report is excluded from the rankings. This guide therefore does not claim that other professions have zero personal bonuses.

The same overview also reports profession-locked cloak embroideries, enchanter-only ring enchants, belt buckles, mounted-speed saddles and an upgradeable alchemy trinket. None of those appear in the profession-specific datamining write-ups that were checked. They are catalogued as unverified claims → rather than repeated as fact.

How to read the data

  • Recipe skill, required character level and item level are different. Camp gates are shown as skill. Equipment milestones use the recipe guide’s listed character level; item level is explicitly labeled wherever used.
  • Some records disagree. The Sharpening Wheel is the clearest example. The database also shows recipe values above 300 and differences between recipe rows and their teaching items. Those are not treated as confirmed unlock requirements.
  • Binding belongs to a specific object. A bind-on-pickup blueprint can teach a bind-on-equip item. Only output-item tooltips establish output binding.
  • Datamined does not mean obtainable. Endgame effects, advanced blueprints, school or terrain bonuses, crowd-control limits and device restrictions still need live checks.
  • Camp buff ≠ personal passive. These are shared benefits and many explicitly exclude a class buff. Raw listed values may scale by character level.
  • Class advice is analysis. It follows the item’s stated purpose, binding and likely use case. It has not been validated with simulations or combat logs.

Scope: the nine primary professions and three secondary skills shown in Forever’s profession sources. Lockpicking and Mage Comprehension are class skills, not extra primary-profession choices. Jewelcrafting and Inscription are not treated as available choices without Forever-specific confirmation.
